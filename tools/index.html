DKD · Tools

Member inventory sheets

First pair your crafting toon in game by typing inv pair <your Discord username> in the chat the Uprising Bot relays (it shows as yourtoon: inv pair you). Pair as many toons as you like; they all feed one sheet. To move a toon to another Discord user, type inv unpair from it first.

Your sheet is encrypted with your own password; nobody else in the guild can open it. There is no password reset. If you forget it, ask an officer to delete the sheet and register again.
